Files supplied with this example:
- destatis-land-use-2024.xlsx: byte-for-byte copy of the official German workbook.
- destatis-land-use-2024.csv: English teaching translation of the complete
  populated range A1:BL18 in worksheet csv-33111-03, in UTF-8 with semicolons
  and decimal commas. All 17 records and 64 columns are retained. Headers,
  survey/table descriptions and region names are translated; identifiers,
  numbers and the original 94 empty cells are preserved. Percentages retain
  the source precision of one decimal place. Trailing empty formatted rows
  are omitted. No missing value is replaced by zero or inferred.
- destatis-land-use-2024-dictionary.csv: complete 64-column crosswalk. The
  english_column field identifies the teaching CSV column; source_column and
  german_label retain the original German header and presentation-table label.
  English meanings, units and original category codes explain each field.
  These are course translations, not official Destatis English translations.

Reproduce the English CSV from the unchanged workbook and column crosswalk,
from the course repository root:
  python3 staging/examples/translate_land_use_2024.py
The converter uses only Python's standard library. It translates survey/table
text and region names and checks source headers, IDs, year and missing cells.
The CSV filename is retained so that existing course download links stay valid.
English region names include Germany, Lower Saxony, North Rhine-Westphalia,
Hesse, Rhineland-Palatinate, Bavaria, Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, Saxony,
Saxony-Anhalt and Thuringia; other names retain their usual spelling.

The csv-33111-03 source worksheet is supplied by Destatis for data processing.
The report explains that its CSV tables omit footnotes and further explanations.
Use the presentation tables, publisher's legend and explanatory sheets alongside
these data. The source description calls the table 33111-3.1; the worksheet/table
navigation labels it 33111-03. Both labels refer to this source table.

The records are Germany (code 0) and 16 federal states (codes 01 to 16), all
at the 2024 reference date. The first five columns describe the source, year
and region. The sixth is total area in hectares. The remaining 58 fields are
shares of total area in percent, at different levels of a category hierarchy.
Do not sum parent categories and their components or combine the national
aggregate with the states. The separate sustainability-indicator tables apply
additional exclusions; their indicator is not a simple sum of all settlement
and transport area. Read their definitions when reproducing that indicator.

Empty source cells do not explain the reason for unavailability. The lesson
imports them as NA. The publisher's zero legend allows positive values smaller
than half a unit in the last displayed digit. Displayed zero and NA must not
be treated as interchangeable. ALKIS became the statistical basis in 2016;
the catalog was expanded in 2023. Historical versions require a comparability
check before combining them with this report.

Data Analysis course preparation (English revision, 26 October 2026):
- prepare-land-use-2024.R selects ten documented English teaching columns,
  assigns shorter analysis names, converts only measurements/year to numeric,
  and excludes Germany
  (code 0). State IDs remain character strings 01-16.
- destatis-land-use-2024-states.rds contains 16 states in 2024. Fields:
  Year, ID, Place, Area_ha, Settlement, Transport, Vegetation, Agriculture,
  Forest, Water. Place uses the English region names from the teaching CSV.
  Area_ha is hectares; the six land-use columns are percentages
  of each state's total area. No imputation or reweighting is applied.
- The four main categories Settlement, Transport, Vegetation and Water are
  a partition up to rounding. Agriculture and Forest are components of
  Vegetation; do not sum both hierarchical levels.
- This Unit-03 snapshot is not the regional multi-year dataset used by the
  later cleaning/regression assignments. It does not include districts or
  an equivalent of the earlier Recreation variable.
- Unit 03's CSV I/O page shows the same preparation before saveRDS().
